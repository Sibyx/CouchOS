# CouchOS privacy policy

*Effective 22 November 2026. Applies to the CouchOS iOS app.*

**CouchOS collects no data.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising
or tracking, and the developer runs no server it reports to.

## What the app does with your information

- **It talks only to your own TV box.** The app finds a CouchOS box on your local
  network (Bonjour) and sends it commands over that network: remote keys, typed
  text, play requests, volume, Bluetooth and TV power. Nothing goes over the
  internet from the app.
- **It uses Plex through your box.** Your box signs in to Plex with the account
  set up on the TV. The phone never sees your Plex sign-in or token. It shows the
  library your box serves it.
- **It keeps a little on your phone:** the box's address, recent searches, your
  sort choice for libraries, and cached artwork. These stay on the device and are
  removed when you delete the app.
- **Local Network permission** is used only to find and control the box.

## TestFlight

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, usage
information and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under
[Apple's TestFlight terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/testflight/sren/terms.html).
The app itself adds nothing to that.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
