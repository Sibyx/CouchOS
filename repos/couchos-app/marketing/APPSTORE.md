## Description (4000)

CouchOS is the remote for a CouchOS TV box: a Raspberry Pi that turns any TV into a calm, simple media centre with Plex, AirPlay and an idle-screen dashboard.

BROWSE ON THE PHONE, WATCH ON THE TV
• Continue Watching, Recently Added and your libraries as poster grids
• Search as you type: movies, shows, episodes, cast, genre or year
• Play on TV, Resume or Play from the start. For a show, carry on with the next episode
• The TV plays it, so your phone never streams and its battery stays out of it

A REMOTE THAT FEELS RIGHT
• An Apple TV-style touchpad: swipe to move, flick to scroll, tap to select, hold for the menu
• Back, Home, play/pause, skip and volume buttons, with haptics on every press
• When the TV asks for text, the keyboard opens on your phone and every keystroke appears on the TV

THE WHOLE TV IN ONE SHEET
• Turn the TV on or put it in standby over HDMI-CEC
• Volume and mute
• Pair and connect Bluetooth speakers
• Stop AirPlay with one tap and Plex is back exactly where it was

FAST, BECAUSE IT'S LOCAL
The box keeps an index of your Plex libraries, so screens open in milliseconds whatever your server's speed. Key presses go straight to the TV over your Wi-Fi.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no cloud and no tracking. The app talks only to your own box on your home network. The box signs in to Plex, so your Plex sign-in never touches the phone.

REQUIREMENTS
A CouchOS box (an open-source Raspberry Pi build, see the website) on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone, and a Plex Media Server.

CouchOS is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Plex, Inc. or the Kodi Foundation.

## App Review notes (4000)

CouchOS is a companion remote for a self-built TV box: a Raspberry Pi running CouchOS (Kodi with Plex, an AirPlay receiver and a small control service). The app finds the box on the local network over Bonjour (_couchos._tcp) and controls it over the LAN. It has no account, no server and no internet backend, and it collects no data.

Without a CouchOS box on the same Wi-Fi network, the app shows "Looking for your CouchOS box" with help on what to check and an option to enter the box's address. That is the expected behaviour, not a crash.

The attached video was recorded on an iPhone 17 Pro with a real box. It shows browsing the Plex library, starting playback on the TV with Play on TV, the touchpad remote, typing into the TV, and the TV, sound and Bluetooth sheet.

The Local Network permission is used only to find and talk to the box. No sign-in is needed. Source code: https://github.com/Sibyx/CouchOS
